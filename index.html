What we do

We build the bridge. You build the business.

VIVNCHA TRADELINK is a business facilitation and market-access company. We help entrepreneurs reach suppliers, manufacturers, distributors and partners in markets they do not yet know.

We start with the Nigeria and Cameroon corridor, where Cameroonian buyers already travel to Nigerian markets. From here we are growing toward Ghana, Togo, Côte d’Ivoire and beyond.

We are
A guide, an introducer and a coordinator for your business in a market you are entering.
We are not
A product seller. We never promise an outcome we cannot control. We promise a clear process and honest effort.

How it works

From request to business connection.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Share your industry, product, goal, quantity and estimated budget.

  2. We assess the assignment

    We confirm whether you need sourcing, scouting, introductions, a visit or representation.

  3. We plan your market route

    We pick the markets, businesses and meetings that match your objective.

  4. We facilitate

    We coordinate the agreed introductions, visits, communication and business activity.

  5. You decide

    You compare the businesses and opportunities and choose what works for you.

  6. We coordinate next steps

    Where agreed, we support follow-up and cross-border coordination.

Clear costs, clear roles

Know what you pay, and who it goes to.

Ours

Our service fee

Quoted in writing for your assignment and paid before work begins. It covers our planning, screening, introductions and coordination.

Third parties

Third-party costs

Transport, accommodation, feeding, shipping, customs and similar costs are listed separately and billed at actual cost.

Yours

Your purchase money

Money for goods stays yours. If you want us to buy on your behalf, that is a separate written agreement with its own terms.

Every assignment starts with a written quote that shows all three, so there are no surprises.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do you sell products?

No. We facilitate. We help you find and connect with suppliers, manufacturers and partners, and you decide what to buy.

Do I have to travel to Nigeria?

No. You can visit with our support, or authorise us to scout the market for you.

How much does it cost?

Every assignment gets a written quote. It separates our service fee from third-party costs and purchase money, so you always know what you are paying for.

Can you buy goods for me?

Only under a separate written agreement that sets out what we buy, how funds are handled and how the goods reach you.

Can you guarantee I will get what I want?

We cannot control a supplier’s decisions or the market. We commit to a transparent process, screened options and honest reporting.

Which markets do you cover?

We launch with Nigeria and Cameroon. Ghana, Togo and Côte d’Ivoire are planned next.
